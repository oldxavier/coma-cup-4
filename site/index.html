About the numbers

How this is made

Source

Every game's recording is replayed in CaptureAge from start to finish, and its live statistics and every unit and building on the map are logged as it plays.

Deaths

Each death is matched to what killed it, so losses to wolves, boar and rhinos, friendly fire and deleted units are counted exactly rather than estimated.

Restored games

A game that was saved and restored is joined into one: nothing is counted twice across the restore.

Names and roles

Players appear under their Liquipedia names; hover a name for the one used in game. Flank or pocket comes from where each player starts.

Knocked out

A player whose units are all gone and who gives no more orders is out. Their eAPM counts only the time they were in the game.

Updates

Every leaderboard is recomputed from all games each time a new series is added.
